## What the exit found ([ADR-0049](../adr/0049-u7-exit-fixes.md))
1. The yes/no model review flagged a plain persona. It now answers a category with a confidence, and only 0.7 or above counts, as a warning.
2. Expiry arrives minutes after a write. The inspector says "pending", and the exit waits for the relayer's value.
3. The inspector hid read failures as an empty list, and the export came back empty. One listing is now shared for 10 seconds, a failure is retried once and then becomes `relayer.slow`, and counts come from the listing.
4. The burner ran low. It was topped up with 0.3 SUI from the spike test key (`6V5c7QEd7HymNpzita52yfExaCLZE23zJ44Ynm2SQQQR`).

Earlier runs reached the hatch (runs 2–4) and the memory controls (runs 3–4) before those fixes landed.
