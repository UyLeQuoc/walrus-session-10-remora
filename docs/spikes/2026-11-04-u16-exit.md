## The services (Part A)
Each one is a real x402 merchant in `apps/merchants` and is listed in the Reef. Each was paid on
testnet on Nov 4, by a Researcher's chat card and by a playground card (`EXAMPLES=1`):

| Service | What it reads | Price (USDC) | A paid receipt (testnet) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wallet X-ray (`wallet`) | An address's coins, each in its own decimals, and the kinds of objects it owns. Read over Sui gRPC | 0.01 | `FZyZ6UQkHA5Lr2rNCvxxLqJE2ZXgDPJiVFBGZuTvUfom` |
| Walrus blob check (`walrus`) | Whether a blob reads (testnet or mainnet aggregator), its size, its kind and its first line | 0.005 | `3xyXHgjyn3eqPDmqUbUU1NCnvmbRQTijSyA8yr6kMa2D` |
| Repo health (`github`) | Stars, forks, open issues, license, archived, last push | 0.01 | `45J3vbtbHiRaMt6yL4w7EZWC65STQts46a24mzMHhQab` |
| Prices (`price`) | SUI, WAL, BTC and ETH in USD from CoinGecko, with the source and time | 0.005 | `GbbeErVYvbvv8tH4tvH4DAYkmRDH5NA9cAuuKUZigpaz` |
| Weather (`weather`) | A city's next three days from Open-Meteo | 0.005 | `6bUwJf2bBc7kQaKF9yGEftUNhd1sRwPRmmUK2VXoUws4` |

**Sources first.** `x402` settles before the handler runs, so a source that failed after payment
would charge for nothing. `sourceFirst` in `@remora/merchant` reads the source in a check before the
payment:
- **First call:** the services that don't need a network read on the first, unpaid call too. So
  does the wallet X-ray, when only one network is served. A source that's down then fails before the
  buyer asks the gas station for anything.
- **Paid call:** served from the merchant's short cache.
- **Retries:** a failed read gets one retry.

The first test run needed this: a weather read failed after sponsorship and held a sponsor gas coin
for 90 s. The next payment then found every coin in use.

**The planner** (`planBuys`, still deterministic). It has one rule per service, and one question can
buy several:
- every 0x id: next to wallet, address, holds or portfolio it's a wallet X-ray; otherwise a Sui risk
  check;
- odds, match, game, bet or fixture: odds;
- a github.com link: the repo's health (never a Brief of it);
- "blob" or "walrus" with a 43-character id: the blob check;
- a price word with SUI, WAL, BTC or ETH: prices;
- a weather word with "in/for <City>": the weather;
- any other link, or summarize, brief or tl;dr: a Brief.

A link ends before any trailing punctuation. Nothing matched means a free answer. Tests:
`runtime.test.ts` ("ADR-0106: one rule per new service…") and `brief-question.test.ts`.

## The Briefer (Part B)
**What it is:** a template `briefer` with a weekday 8am `cron` schedule and the Researcher's payees.
- **Its list** ("SUI and WAL prices", "weather in Hanoi", a wallet, a repo) becomes one question the
  planner reads (`brief-question.ts`). The question never says "brief", so the planner never buys a
  Brief of the question itself.
- **On its schedule or on Run now,** it asks through the Researcher's pay path and posts one brief.
  Each purchase gets its own receipt, and the brief goes to Telegram too when Telegram is linked.
- **In the web:** a third card in the wizard (with "What to follow"), a page (its list, schedule and
  next run, Run now, the last brief with its receipts), a card in What's a remora? and a line in the
  guide.

**Its run** (`EXAMPLES=1`, Nov 4): "Briefer · examples" followed prices, the weather in Hanoi and
MystenLabs/walrus. Run now paid three services, and each receipt verified through `/api/verify`:
- weather, `BstmbczJULMFY276oEeD6wFWAw9UhL97mEjMVb263KXG`;
- repo health, `EhUgsymNaACVRgaFAoQ283zbLPiFAQ3zNZkimzpbsYy1`;
- prices, `Dva51mdPk1JoMG5ydFcmX2TxKMimp1qEUwAzonwy3EtZ`.

**Contract changes** (ADR-0107: same PR, listed here):
- `TeamRemora.items` in `packages/schema/src/team.ts`: a Briefer's list in team files, at most 6.
- Nothing else in `packages/schema` changed. The Briefer's list and schedule live in the remora's
