## A. An open Reef
### A1. A remora sells its answers ([ADR-0112](../adr/0112-remoras-on-sale.md))
- **Settle after:** `@remora/merchant`'s `x402` gained `settle: "after"`. It verifies, runs the
  handler, and settles only on a 2xx. If settlement fails, the answer is replaced by the 402. Tests
  are in `packages/merchant/src/settle-after.test.ts`.
- **The listing:** a remora on sale is `reef:remora-<slug>`, which fits the `ReefRef` contract. It's
  paid at its owner's address at `/api/x402/r/<slug>`, and its sale lives in `config.sell`.
- **A sold turn:**
  - reads only `sources` and `feedback`, never `me`;
  - has chain read tools only;
  - can't buy a remora (`sale.depth`).

  An owner's remoras don't pay each other (`sale.self`). "ask @slug" in the planner hands the whole
  question over.
- **The web:** a **Sell its answers** card, a **Buy from other remoras** card (one `set_payees`
  signature), and "→ it paid …" under the buyer's payment.
- **Exit** (`ECONOMY=1`, passed Nov 4): the burner's Researcher sold as `@uy-researcher` for 0.01.
  burner2's Researcher was allowed to pay it with one signature, then asked "ask @uy-researcher is
  package 0x2 safe to depend on?":

  | Who paid | What | Amount | Digest |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | burner2's Researcher | `@uy-researcher` (its owner's address) | 0.01 USDC | `7vkXZTwFPcDBrt6QkvNZZLrw5C9UQNUHUFHePvEVSGGb` |
  | `@uy-researcher` | Sui risk check | 0.01 USDC | `5pXu4RPf7SceRh7VeNuNARm95B399NuzoTXxowWT3d5B` |

  Both checked out through `/api/verify`, and the seller's page showed the sale with the buyer's
  digest.

### A2 and A3. Anyone lists an API; anyone settles through us ([ADR-0113](../adr/0113-community-listings-and-public-facilitator.md))
- **Listing:** `/reef/new` probes an x402 URL with one unpaid GET. It must ask for exact
  `sui:testnet` USDC, at most 0.1. The owner signs a message naming what was found. The listing is
  `reef:community-<slug>`, stored in one of the owner's remoras' config, so there's no schema push.
- **The public facilitator:** `/api/facilitator` forwards to the in-container facilitator. It's
  testnet only, with 200 settlements per `payTo` a day and 10 gas calls per address a minute.
- **The example:** [`examples/sell-an-api`](../../examples/sell-an-api) is a haiku merchant in about
  20 lines, with tests.
- **Exit** (`ECONOMY=1`, passed Nov 4):
  - the example ran locally, paid to burner2 and settling through this server's
    `/api/facilitator`;
  - the burner listed it as `@haiku`, signing in the test wallet;
  - a Researcher was allowed to pay it and asked "ask @haiku walrus memory";
  - it paid 0.005 USDC, `GQR9nDvQa692eJqrafZMAD9Juq6BteDcN4kKKBjKyyWf`, which verified.

## B. Crews ([ADR-0114](../adr/0114-crews.md))
- **The team file:** it gains `crew.lead`, and each member gains `covers`.
- **The lead:** it plans the buys and hands each to the member that covers it, all at once. It pays
  nothing and answers with a line per receipt.
- **The Crew card:** spend per member and in total, pause together (runtime), and retire together
  (one revoke transaction).

## D. Numbers under load
**The run:** `LOAD=1 bun scripts/load-run.ts 50 1000` in `apps/server`, Nov 4, 15:13–16:11 UTC. The
raw numbers are in [2026-11-04-u17-load.json](2026-11-04-u17-load.json).
- **The remoras:** 50 Researchers made in five teams of ten, one burner signature each:
  `7go4gxcR…`, `6MZvAY75…`, `87FYqcR4…`, `3w3ZtDq6…`, `HzbQKkNa…`. Each had 0.30 USDC a month and
  0.01 a payment.
- **What ran:** the product's own pay path (`requestPayment`: the ADR-0109 guard, Sonar's
  deterministic policy, the Disc, the gas station, the facilitator, the merchants, receipts), with
  **no model**: no answer model and no Sonar check model. The script ran its own facilitator and
  merchants on 8898 and 8899.
- **The node:** Tatum answered `402` throughout, so every Sui call went to the public fullnode
  (ADR-0097).

| At once | Calls | Paid | Payments a minute | p50 | p95 | p99 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 40 | 40 | 16.4 | 3.7 s | 4.7 s | 5.9 s |
| 5 | 160 | 160 | 22.4 | 13.3 s | 14.0 s | 15.1 s |
| 10 | 300 | 300 | 22.4 | 26.7 s | 28.0 s | 31.1 s |
| 20 | 500 | 500 | 21.8 | 53.3 s | 62.8 s | 79.0 s |

- **Paid:** **1,000 of 1,000**, for 5.83 USDC. The owner's balance and the Discs' onchain spend both
  moved by exactly 5.83.
- **Must fail:** **50 of 50 stopped onchain**, with the safety check turned off so the Disc itself had
  to stop them. The gas station's simulation hit the Move aborts:
  - 25 to a service that asks to be paid elsewhere: `EPayeeNotAllowed`;
  - 25 over the per-payment cap: `EOverPaymentCap`.

  No money moved.
- **Overspends:** **0**. Every Disc's allowance was read back over gRPC, and none spent more than its
  limit or paid above its cap.
- **Sponsored gas:** 1.0036 SUI in all, about **0.001 SUI a payment**.
- **Failures by code:** none among the paid calls.
- **What it shows:** throughput is flat at about 22 a minute from 5 to 20 at once, and latency grows
  with concurrency, at about 2.7 s per payment in the queue. So something takes payments one at a
  time. Two suspects:
  - every remora drew from **one owner's address balance** (the burner);
  - every call went through the public fullnode.

  The run doesn't tell them apart. Real owners pay from their own balances, so the next run should
  use several owners.
- **After:** the 50 were retired, ten per signature.
- **D3:** `/api/stats` gives the rails per network from the DB (remoras, paid calls, USDC moved,
  receipts matched onchain, blocked attempts, sales) plus this run's summary. `/stats` shows it,
  with nothing typed in by hand.
