# U17 landing review: Network section, nav, Jobs and Proof

Reviewer: a fresh judge persona. Three minutes, never heard of Remora, Disc or x402. Source: the r17 screenshots only (desktop 1440, Pixel 7, desktop with reduced motion).

## Five-second read

"Remoras pay remoras. Anyone can join." lands as a headline, and the first card works. The indented "↳ @uy-researcher → Sui risk check" row makes a two-hop payment readable as a chain: who paid whom, 0.01 USDC each, with a hash link. After that the section relies on names the page never explains: "Reef", "facilitator", "Disc", "x402" and "the list". The crew card shows three flat rows ("Code → Repo health", "Markets → Prices", "Onchain → Sui risk check"). It does not show the one question, the lead or the split, so "a crew" doesn't come across. The "at scale" band has five large numbers and no label. What was measured, plus the caveat that undercuts "4.7s" ("p95 63 s at 20"), sits in a dim monospace footnote that a judge in a hurry will skip. Two claims contradict the "What's real, what isn't." section further down. "Pause everything from Telegram" and the "Telegram Mini App" chip clash with "Not yet: … the Telegram Mini App". "@haiku, an API an owner listed" clashes with "All eight paid services are our demo merchants". The new desktop nav link works and follows page order, but the phone header has no section links at all.
