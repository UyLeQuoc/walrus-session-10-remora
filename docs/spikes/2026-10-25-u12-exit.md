## Verified, and not
- ✅ **Every agent action stays inside its onchain limit, or goes back to its owner:**
  - Remora's Disc and over-limit approvals;
  - Kitty's delegate cap and quorum;
  - Receipts' signature, delegation and anchor before lock.
- ✅ **Keys stay where they belong.** No key leaves its holder:
  - the stdio MCP servers sign on the agent's side;
  - the remote endpoints are read-only (Receipts, Kitty) or scoped by token (Remora);
  - tokens are stored as SHA-256.
- ❌ **Over the Disc, Remora refuses before signing.** It reads the live Disc's cap, so the chain never
  sees that payment. The onchain abort over the Disc was proven in U3 and is covered by the evals.
- ❌ **Only test wallets have signed.** In all three apps that's an injected test wallet or a generated
  key. No real wallet app has signed yet.
- ✅ **The remote `/mcp` endpoints of Receipts and Kitty were checked with a live HTTP client** (03:52
  UTC), each against its running server, with a token signed into existence by a test wallet:
  - **Receipts:** a fresh group "MCP live check (test)", `e681267d…`.
    - `initialize` answers `receipts` 0.2.0, and `tools/list` shows only `list_markets`, `get_call`,
      `get_receipt` and `leaderboard`.
    - `list_markets` returns the group's real markets.
    - `get_receipt` for the forecaster's pick, which is in another group, is refused: "This token can
      only read its own group".
    - No token gets 401. Revoking with a signature, then calling again, gets 401.
  - **Kitty:** Bao (test) on the exit pot `0x85a8…1967`.
    - `initialize` answers `kitty` 0.2.0, and `tools/list` shows only `pot_status`, `why` and `norms`.

    - `approve` over the remote endpoint is refused ("Unknown tool").

  Both take bearer tokens only (no OAuth).
