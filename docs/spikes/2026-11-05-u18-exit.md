## C. Easier to steer
### C1. Describe the job ([ADR-0123](../adr/0123-describe-the-job-and-chat-commands.md))
- **The job:** "Every weekday at 8, tell me if any of my wallets moved more than 50 USDC". The
  draft was a valid **Wallet watcher**, a reasonable reading.
- **It hatched** through the ordinary create (`e8f0b8d1…`, no signature, since it never pays) and
  is live in practice mode.
- **Refused before any model call:** "trade my coins for me every hour": "Remoras don't trade or
  swap coins…".

### C2. Chat commands
- "rename Researcher · u18 to Researcher · u18x" in the web thread made a change card, and Apply
  renamed it.

  limit read back as 200000.
