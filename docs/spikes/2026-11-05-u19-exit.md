**The goal:** [U19](../goals/uy-19-it-gets-better.md).
**The decisions:**
- [ADR-0134](../adr/0134-u19-it-gets-better-and-anyone-can-check.md): the goal;
- [ADR-0135](../adr/0135-memwal-sdk-0-1-12-index-check.md) to [ADR-0150](../adr/0150-template-updates.md): one per part.

**Branch:** `u19-better`, built in a git worktree. Nine parts were built in parallel by sub-agents
in the same worktree (B2, C4, D1, D4, D5; then B1, A3+A4, C3, D2+D3) while the lead built A0–A2, C1
and C2; the runs used a second, clean worktree so edits in progress never reached a running stack.

Everything below ran on Sui testnet, against the local stack (`U19=1`, `e2e/tests/u19.spec.ts`)
unless it says production. Each digest opens on Suiscan. The results files are
`e2e/exit-results/u19.json`, `u19-prod.json` and `u19-live.json` (gitignored).

## A. It gets better, and shows why
### A0. The SDK and the index check ([ADR-0135](../adr/0135-memwal-sdk-0-1-12-index-check.md))
- `@mysten-incubation/memwal` pinned to 0.1.12. Its `listMemories()` and our signed listing agreed on
  the operator account: 746 memories in 320 namespaces, the same blob ids (610 ms vs 264 ms).
- **The index check**, after the A run: `sources` 10 written, 10 listed; `receipts` 3 and 3; missing
  0. The Memory Inspector shows "written 13 · on Walrus Memory 13 · missing 0"; `diagnose` checks
  the last 7 days.
- **`dedupe: true`, measured** on 20 lines (10 new, then 5 exact repeats and 5 paraphrases, after
  the first ten landed): our recall-based dedupe skipped 5 + 5 and stored 9; the relayer's
  `dedupe: true` gave 15 distinct jobs and stored 15 (exact repeats only). Kept ours.

   odds `544VM2HU…`, prices `5EEeTfF1…`). The owner rated them: three odds answers that named no team
