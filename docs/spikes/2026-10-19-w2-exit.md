## Findings

- **Every Tatum stream was cut every 30 s.** The transport's per-call deadline applied to `SubscribeCheckpoints` too. A ping that landed in a gap was lost once. Streams now have no deadline, and the watcher backfills missed checkpoints after any reconnect (ADR-0035). This also explains the W1 indexer's "reconnects".

- **The sponsor had one gas coin.** Two concurrent sponsored payments (a Scout's first run and its first question) collided on it, and one got a 402. The gas station now holds a coin per transaction (ADR-0035). The testnet sponsor was split into 6 coins (`6Qsqg7rJwVBrCPDo4eGYhMMzmbKUGjcuxVWKh9XX4nJ8`).
- **That 402 was recorded as "Paid".** Fixed: an unsettled payment is `blocked` (`x402.unsettled`).
- **The per-IP ceiling (30/min) locked the W2 pages out** because they poll. The W2 reads are now unmetered (ADR-0035).
- **After a reload, the wallet is gone** (no auto-connect) while the session stays. The pages now say "Connect wallet to sign".
- **`OPENROUTER_API_KEY` in `.env` is a placeholder (401).** Every model call failed, and the runtime fell back as designed:
  - Scout answered from what it bought;
  - Sonar ran its deterministic policy (the check model can only tighten, and it was down);
  - rules came from the phrase parser.

  The exit passes without a model, but model-written answers are unverified until a real key is set.
- **MemWal `stats` and the owner listing lag behind recall.** The rule blob was recallable while `stats` said 0. The F6 warning says writes take time to land. Don't treat a fresh mismatch as a loss.
- **The memory port's redactor (F11a, `hex-key-64`) strips 64-hex strings, including intent hashes inside a Wake stored in memory.** The DB receipt keeps them, and G3 verifies from it. An owner-held Wake should keep its hashes; this is for W3.
- **Tatum streams worked unchanged.** The watcher re-subscribes when the watched set changes (30 s refresh); the first event landed 4.7 s after finality.
