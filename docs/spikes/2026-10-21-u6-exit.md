## What the exit found ([ADR-0045](../adr/0045-u6-exit-fixes.md))
1. The check model failed its schema. It ran on the fallback model, whose reasons were too long, and it blocked a legitimate purchase. It now defaults to `LLM_MODEL`, and the reason length is unbounded.
2. YAML read an unquoted `0x…` address as a number. Hex scalars now stay the source string.
3. Confirm ran before Tatum saw the transaction. Owner transactions are now re-read for up to 12 tries, and the import page offers "Check again".
4. The burner ran out of gas. It was topped up with 0.3 SUI from the spike test key (`DpXwQHbvAZSvaTzjVLUcRxmiEWAC1ffBRSMsrnFbBDPB`).
5. A room's signal went to an old Scout. It now goes to the room's lead, else the newest remora of that template.
6. A revoked Disc failed silently. It's now a plain `allowance.revoked` block, nothing is signed, and the remora's status is left alone.
7. The D1 watcher broke past 10 watched addresses ("filter contains 11 literals"). Past 10 it streams unfiltered and filters locally. On Tatum testnet that's about 3 checkpoints a second.
8. The paid answer after Pay once ran on the default model while its Wake named Scout's model. It now runs on the remora's model, and the Wake records the model that actually answered.
9. Answers made up their cost ("$0.0001", "1 reef"). Bought data now carries its price into the prompt.
10. A self-asked answer read as the reply to the owner's line. It's labelled "Re: <question>" now, and bubbles render Markdown.
