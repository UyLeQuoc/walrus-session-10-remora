# U18 landing review: "It learns", "Everyday money", nav

Reviewer persona: a hackathon judge with three minutes who has never heard of Remora, x402, MPP, Seal or Walrus. Based only on the screenshots in `shots-u18/` and `crops-u18/`.

## Five-second read

**"Everyday money" works.** Each card is a short story with a person in it ("Errand → Linh, "lunch" 0.10 USDC", "You said yes → Linh, "dinner" 0.30 USDC", "You accepted 0.006 → it paid the new price") and a receipt on every row. A judge gets "it pays people, asks before anything unusual, and every payment is onchain" in one pass.

**"It learns" doesn't land yet.** The headline promises learning. The cards show:
- a weekly review that says "I was taught nothing this week" and that two purchases were "not useful";
- Reef chips that read only "sui-risk: new · github: new";
- a limit of 0.20 in the review, which contradicts the 0.03 → 0.09 raise in the card next to it.

Nothing on screen shows the remora doing something differently because of what it learned.

**"You sign every change" is the key safety claim, and it is worded five different ways** across the two sections:
- "One signature accepted it"
- "Accepted with one signature"
- "Taken back, one signature"
- "You said yes"
- "You accepted"

None of them says that you sign with your wallet.

**Two layout problems on phone:** the nav shows no section links, and the receipt links are small to tap.
