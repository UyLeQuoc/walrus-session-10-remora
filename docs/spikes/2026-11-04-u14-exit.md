# U14 exit: a visual-first web (Nov 4, 2026)

**The goal:** [U14](../goals/uy-14-visual-ui-pass.md). **The decision:**
[ADR-0103](../adr/0103-visual-first-ui-pass.md). **The result:**
[ADR-0104](../adr/0104-visual-first-result.md).

**Where it ran:** the branch `u14-visual-ui`, from the tag `pre-u14-ui` (f2a29d3), merged through
PR #19. The review notes are in [`docs/ux/review/`](../ux/review/README.md).

**When:** Nov 4, well inside the time box. The freeze date was Nov 7, 12:00 Vietnam time.

## What was built
- **The review capture.** `UX=1` adds `ux-desktop` and `ux-phone` to the audit spec.
  - It shoots 73 states on each device: filled, empty, signed out, loading, error, and the landing
    page by section.
  - It counts the interface words, and `sheet.py` makes contact sheets and the word table.
- **The visual kit** in `apps/web/src/components/visual/` (MIT), shown at `/lab/visual`.
  - It's built on `motion`, `lucide-react`, `recharts` and inline SVG, with no new dependency.
  - It has 14 component tests, and reduced motion shows each end state.
- **Every page** got the kit, a one-line purpose, its explanations behind `?` hints, skeletons
  of the final shape, and one `Problem` card for errors.
- **The demo video** was re-captured, its captions put through the rubric, and rendered again.
- **The landing page's screens** were re-shot, with Pixel 7 versions for phones.

## The video
- **Re-captured** with `DEMO=1 --project=demo` on Nov 4, from the frozen app.
- **The storyboard:**
  - every caption is ten words or fewer;
  - the playground chapter ends on the payment's receipt, verified;
  - the teach chapter ends on the ignored transfer;
  - the close gives getremora.vercel.app/playground.
- **Length:** 161.1 s, inside the 150–180 s the form asks for.
- **Render:** `render.ts --workers 8` took 545 s here (9,666 frames, at about 24 fps). It wrote:
  - `out/remora-demo.mp4`: the master, 1080p60, 96.8 MB;
  - `out/remora-demo-web.mp4`: the share copy, 1080p30, 13.2 MB, which is also the landing page's
    video.

  No mark or box was missing.
- **Checked after the render:** a frame sheet of the final MP4. It shows the new UI: the receipt
  card, the cap meters on approvals and Funds' meters.

## Data the visuals wanted that the API doesn't return
- A memory row's text, without searching.
- A public receipt to try Verify with, for a visitor who has none.
- When a status problem started.
- A way to issue a new Disc for a remora whose Disc is gone onchain (Change budget revokes the old
  one and reissues).
- The learning rate's count of failed purchases, so a failed answer isn't "answered from memory".

## Left for after the submission (minors and nits)
- **Light theme:** disabled buttons, the selected kind card, and the two styles of segmented control.
- **Layout:** uneven input widths; Connections' cards could be single rows.
- **The guide** has no contents list; Services and Status need more polish.
- **The video:**
  - a clean demo account, without the burner's old remoras and approvals;
  - zoom on each fact;
  - footage of the aborted payment;
  - the cost card in USDC.
