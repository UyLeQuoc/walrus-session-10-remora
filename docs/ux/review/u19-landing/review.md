# Landing review: U19 round ("better", "check", four new money cards, nav)

Reviewer: a fresh judge who has never heard of Remora, Disc, x402, MPP, Seal or Walrus. Sources: the eight screenshots only (desktop 1440 wide, Pixel 7).

## Five-second read

"The same ten questions, three times. With today's memory it buys less, and says why." lands in one pass. The bar chart reads as "full, full, about 40 %", so a skimmer comes away with "it learned to buy less", which is the point. Two things undercut it on a second look. Memory at T₀ is identical to No memory, and the page never says why that control is there; the 7-minute window between T₀ (07:18) and the replay (07:25) also contains a "daily check". "Don't trust us. Check the receipts." is a strong heading, and the auditor chain (you signed, then ok, then you signed, then blocked) reads as a clear story. After that the section switches to Merkle roots, Anchored events, intent hashes and package names. "Verify one yourself" has nothing to click, and its numbers (29 Wakes, 14 receipts, 31 Wakes, "a week") don't line up with the Researcher in the section above. Of the four new money cards, "Pay a name" and "A bill from a photo" read in one pass, "Treasurer" mostly does, and "Any x402 agent can find the Reef" doesn't, because the title is two unknown words. Layout holds at both widths: no overflow, and sampled text contrast is at least 7:1. On phone, though, the inline "receipt ↗", "memory ↗" and "tx ↗" links are small, and the header shows none of the new nav links.
